<h3>Quiz 5, item 5: your repertoire piece. Record it, or play it in Clef Runner.</h3>
<p>Choose from the <a href="https://keys-to-success-coursepack.netlify.app/handouts/MUSC1105_Quiz5_Repertoire.pdf">Quiz 5 Repertoire page</a>, which links my practice video for each piece:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Reinagle, 24 Short and Easy Pieces, Op. 1:</strong> TWO pieces from Nos. 1 to 12, or ONE piece from Nos. 13 to 24.</li>
<li><strong>Or ONE of the other pieces on the page:</strong> Gurlitt, Vivace in C major; Gurlitt, Through Forest and Field; the Minuet in G major (measures 1 to 16); Beethoven, Ecossaise in E-flat major; Haydn, German Dance in B-flat major.</li>
</ul>
<p>Play the whole piece, both hands, in the printed key, with the repeats. A video is graded on accuracy and on keeping a steady pulse; Clef Runner grades accuracy at the tempo you choose.</p>

<h4>Way 1: Record a video (any piece on the page)</h4>
<p>Record yourself playing on a piano or keyboard, with your hands and the keyboard in the frame. One take per piece is fine; for two Reinagle pieces, one video with both or two videos. Choose a tempo you can keep steady from the first note to the last.</p>
<p>To submit: upload the video file (or files) under <strong>File Upload</strong>, or paste a link to an unlisted YouTube video under <strong>Text Entry</strong>. No cloud drive links. Say the piece (and its number, for Reinagle) at the start.</p>

<h4>Way 2: Play it in Clef Runner (Reinagle, the Minuet in G or the Ecossaise)</h4>
<p>Clef Runner: <a href="https://clef-runner.netlify.app">https://clef-runner.netlify.app</a>. <strong>You need a MIDI keyboard and a computer:</strong> Chrome, Edge or Firefox with your keyboard plugged in. Safari and iPads will not work. Every station in Room 269 has one.</p>
<ol>
<li>Choose your player profile, or make one. Your progress lives in that profile, in that browser, on that computer, so use the same one each time.</li>
<li>From the main menu press <strong>Homework</strong>, then open <strong>Quiz 5: Repertoire</strong>, the first card.</li>
<li>Pick a tempo (<strong>60, 80 or 100</strong>) and press <strong>Play</strong> beside a piece. It always uses both hands on the grand staff in the printed key, and the app plays the repeats for you, so a run is the whole piece. Prefer a page that stays still? Choose <strong>View: Page</strong>; it grades the same.</li>
<li>Practice as much as you like. Only your best run on each piece counts. To practice slower than 60, play the piece in Repertoire (under Quiz 5 Repertoire) at 40; those runs are practice and do not count.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>How the app grades it.</strong> A run counts at tempo 60 or faster. Option 1 is the average of your best runs on TWO DIFFERENT pieces from Nos. 1 to 12, so until you play a second one it counts 0 (one 90% piece alone is 45). Option 2 is your best run on one piece: Reinagle Nos. 13 to 24, the Minuet in G (measures 1 to 16) or the Ecossaise. Your grade is the higher option, so trying both never hurts. There is no minimum: a grade of 85 is 17 out of 20.</p>
<p>To submit: on the same screen, scroll down, type your first name, last name and school ID, press <strong>Get Code</strong>, then <strong>Copy Code</strong>, and paste ONLY the code, all of it, from KTS1 to the last letter, under <strong>Text Entry</strong>. Use the school ID your gradebook knows you by.</p>

<h4>Submit one way. Your last submission is the one graded.</h4>
<p>A video and a code are worth the same 20 points. If you submit more than once, only the last submission counts.</p>

<h4>Due Sunday, November 8, 2026, 11:59 PM Eastern. You may turn it in early.</h4>
