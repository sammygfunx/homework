===HTML===
<p>Compose a short pop tune of your own: a melody with lyrics over a chord progression. A verse or a chorus is enough. This builds on <a href="https://keys-to-success-coursepack.netlify.app/handouts/MUSC1105_Pop_Inversions.pdf">Pop Progressions with Inversions</a> and item 4 of Quiz 5: the same chords in the closest inversions, now under a tune of your own.</p>
<p><strong>Due Sunday, November 15, 2026, at 11:59 PM Eastern.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Start here:</strong> <a href="https://keys-to-success-coursepack.netlify.app/handouts/MUSC1105_Pop_Tune.pdf">the Pop Tune Composition sheet</a> (the checklist, how to start, and a worked example) and <a href="https://keys-to-success-coursepack.netlify.app/handouts/MUSC1105_Pop_Tune_Template.pdf">the template</a> (fill it in, or use staff paper).</p>

<h4>Checklist: your score has</h4>
<ol>
<li><strong>At least four chords</strong> in your progression.</li>
<li><strong>The melody,</strong> notated in the treble clef.</li>
<li><strong>Lyrics</strong> under the melody that teach something you have learned about the piano this semester.</li>
<li><strong>Chord symbols</strong> above the staff, each one over the note where its chord begins.</li>
<li><strong>Roman numerals</strong> below the staff.</li>
<li><strong>Accompaniment, root position:</strong> the left hand plays the roots and the right hand plays root-position triads.</li>
<li><strong>Accompaniment, closest inversions:</strong> the left hand plays the roots and the right hand plays each chord in the inversion closest to the one before it.</li>
<li><strong>A rhythm</strong> for the right-hand chords that gives your progression its own character.</li>
</ol>

<h4>Ways to write it (all equal)</h4>
<ul>
<li>By hand on staff paper or on the template, then upload a photo or a scan.</li>
<li>MuseScore (free, at musescore.org) or any notation app, then upload a PDF or an image.</li>
<li>Krill Keys shows your notes on a grand staff and names each chord as you play it, and Lead Sheet Lab drills the chord symbols. Use them to help; submit the score itself.</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>To submit:</strong> upload your score under <strong>File Upload</strong> (one file, or a few photos of the pages). Make sure every page is readable. Your last submission is the one graded.</p>

2. WHAT TO SAY IN CLASS (about ninety seconds)
===========================================================

"Your homework this week is a pop tune of your own: a short melody with
lyrics, over four chords or more. A verse or a chorus is plenty.

It builds on the pop progressions with inversions from Week 11 and on
Quiz 5: the same chords in the closest inversions, now under your own tune.

The sheet on Canvas has an eight-item checklist, and that checklist is the
grade: four chords; the melody in treble clef; lyrics about something you
learned about the piano this semester; chord symbols above; Roman numerals
below; the accompaniment in root position; then in the closest inversions;
and a rhythm for the right hand.

Write it however you like: staff paper, the template, MuseScore, any app.
Upload a photo or a PDF. There is a worked example on the sheet.

There is no performance this time. Due Sunday at 11:59 PM, the same night
as Choice Board 3."
