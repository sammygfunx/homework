<h3>Choice Board 2: Triad Qualities. Pick ONE way below and submit only that one.</h3>
<p>This homework practices major, minor, diminished and augmented triads. Every way below is worth the same grade. You may try more than one for practice, but submit ONE.</p>

<h4>Way 1: Da Capo (the reading game)</h4>
<p>Open <a href="https://da-capo-game.netlify.app">https://da-capo-game.netlify.app</a>. On the title screen choose <strong>HOMEWORK</strong>, then select <strong>TRIAD QUALITIES</strong>. That starts the run. A run in any other course does not count for this board.</p>
<p><strong>Full credit when EITHER is true:</strong> you beat all 24 stages, <em>or</em> you have cleared 50 cards. The game counts for you: GET CODE (pause menu, or the screen at the end) says whether you have earned credit yet and, if not, how many more cards you need.</p>
<p>Play on a laptop or desktop (the letters A to G play notes; tap T for sharp or R for flat before a letter for a black key; press letters together for notes stacked on top of each other), on an iPad with the on-screen keys, or with a MIDI keyboard. Use the SAME browser on the SAME device each time and press <strong>CONTINUE</strong> to pick a run back up.</p>
<p><strong>To submit:</strong> GET CODE, type your first name, last name and school ID, COPY CODE, and paste ALL of it (from KTS1 to the last letter) under <strong>Text Entry</strong>. Paste only the code.</p>

<h4>Way 2: Piano Tennis</h4>
<p>Open <a href="https://piano-tennis.netlify.app">https://piano-tennis.netlify.app</a> in Chrome or Edge on a computer. Choose <strong>VS COMPUTER</strong>, and in the <strong>Difficulty tier</strong> list choose the tier whose name ends 'Triads: All Qualities'. Type your name, then <strong>START MATCH</strong>.</p>
<p><strong>Full credit:</strong> [FILL IN: what counts, e.g. win one match on that tier].</p>
<p>A MIDI keyboard works in Chrome or Edge. Without one, your computer keyboard is a piano: A S D F G H J K are white keys, W E T Y U are black keys, Z and X move the octave. Piano Tennis does not run on a phone or an iPad.</p>
<p><strong>To submit:</strong> on the screen at the end of the match press <strong>COPY SCORE REPORT (FOR CANVAS)</strong> and paste the whole report under <strong>Text Entry</strong>.</p>

<h4>Way 3: Chord Crush</h4>
<p>Open <a href="https://chord-crush-mobile.netlify.app">https://chord-crush-mobile.netlify.app</a>. On the title screen choose <strong>Chord Stages</strong> and play the four triad stages: Major Triads, Minor Triads, Diminished Triads, Augmented Triads.</p>
<p><strong>Full credit:</strong> [FILL IN: what counts, e.g. clear N levels in each of those stages, root position or any position].</p>
<p>Chord Crush works with a MIDI keyboard (Chrome, Edge or Firefox on a computer) or by touch on an iPad or a phone. A mouse plays one note at a time, so it cannot play a chord.</p>
<p><strong>To submit:</strong> on the title screen choose <strong>Statistics</strong>, then <strong>Canvas Code</strong>. Type your first name, last name and school ID, press <strong>Generate code</strong>, then <strong>Copy code</strong>, and paste the code under <strong>Text Entry</strong>. Use the same browser and device you practiced on: the code reports everything that profile has played.</p>

<h4>Way 4: Record a video</h4>
<p>Record yourself at the piano playing these <strong>twelve triads</strong> in root position, blocked (all three notes together), and <strong>say the name of each one out loud</strong> as you play it, for example <em>"F, A-flat, C-flat: F diminished."</em> On C, then on F, then on G, play a major, a minor, a diminished and an augmented triad.</p>
<p>Keep your hands and the keyboard in the frame and your voice clear. One take is fine. <strong>To submit:</strong> upload the video file under <strong>File Upload</strong>, or paste a link to an unlisted YouTube video under <strong>Text Entry</strong>. No cloud drive links.</p>

<h4>Submit one way. Your last submission is the one graded.</h4>
