===HTML===
<h3>Choose ONE option and submit ONLY ONE. Either option earns full credit for HW #5.</h3>
<p>Pick the video if you'd rather play one tune you know well on a real piano and be done in one take. Pick Orbit if you'd rather drill the scale degrees with the game checking you, or you don't have a piano handy: Orbit runs on any laptop and needs no MIDI keyboard. They're worth the same. Submit one. You may do both for extra practice if you like, but only one submission is counted.</p>

<h4>Option A - Record a video: one melody in C, G and F</h4>
<p>Pick ONE melody. Either one of the six from this week's handout (Mary Had a Little Lamb, The Itsy Bitsy Spider, Jingle Bells, Vivaldi's Spring, Twinkle Twinkle, Happy Birthday), or any melody you love that you have written out in scale degrees yourself: a TV theme, a movie theme, a pop song, something from your own repertoire. If you bring your own, say the title and the artist on camera before you play, and hold your scale-degree sheet up to the camera (or attach a photo of it alongside the video).</p>
<p>Record yourself playing it, right hand alone, in C major, then G major, then F major. That is Orbit's order too. Say the key out loud before each one. One continuous video, not three clips. If you stumble, start that key over and keep the camera rolling. A steady tempo, and slow is fine. No metronome required.</p>
<p>Keep it under 3 minutes. Your hand and the keyboard need to be visible. A phone propped on the music stand is fine.</p>
<p>Fingering is not graded on this one. What is: the right notes in all three keys, and a steady beat from the first note to the last in each key. <strong>Grading is based on accuracy: the right scale degrees in every key, at a steady tempo.</strong></p>
<p>To submit: upload the video file in the <strong>File Upload</strong> tab below, or paste a link to an unlisted YouTube video in the <strong>Website URL</strong> tab. No cloud drive links.</p>

<h4>Option B - Play Orbit: THE FIRST THREE STARS</h4>
<p>Orbit is the transposition trainer: <a href="https://orbit-transpose.netlify.app">https://orbit-transpose.netlify.app</a></p>
<p><strong>You do not need a MIDI keyboard.</strong> Click the on-screen keys with the mouse, or play your computer keyboard: the Z row (Z X C V B N M , . /) is the white keys starting on C, the S D G H J L ; keys are the black keys, and [ and ] move the octave down and up. A MIDI keyboard works too if you have one, and the Room 269 keyboards plug right in. Use a laptop or desktop in a current browser.</p>
<ol>
<li>On the first screen pick your name, or type a new one and press <strong>Begin</strong>. That profile is where your progress lives.</li>
<li>Press <strong>✦ Homework</strong> in the header (it is on every screen), or <strong>✦ Homework: The First Three Stars</strong> on the Star Map.</li>
</ol>
<p>The homework screen is a checklist: C major, G major and F major, each with a DRILL box and a MELODY box. In the game's own words: "For each key: drill its scale degrees (15 or more tries at 75% or better), then learn one of the six coursepack melodies in that key (pass the Slow tempo). Orbit lights the stars in this order: C first, then G, then F." The <strong>Drill →</strong> and <strong>Melodies →</strong> buttons on each row take you straight there. G stays LOCKED until C is done, and F until G is done.</p>
<p>The drill is Find the Note or Name the Note, your choice; both count. The 75% is your running accuracy in that key over everything you've played there, so slow and right beats fast and wrong. The melody box needs one of the six handout tunes passed in that key at the Slow tempo (the melody list marks the six with ✦ HW #5; any other tune still unlocks stars but does not earn the homework). Passing is 85% in Perform: after a two-bar count-in the clock runs, every note has a window, and a note is either the wrong pitch or late, never both. The tempo slider is yours, but credit goes to the slowest speed you used during the take: stay at or above the Slow tempo the whole way and it "counts as Slow"; dip below it and the results say "Practice tempo - no mastery credit", nothing recorded.</p>
<p>Your progress saves in that browser, on that computer. Use the same browser and the same computer every time, and pick the same name. Clearing your browser's site data wipes it.</p>
<p>The verdict line at the bottom of the checklist always tells you where you stand: "FULL CREDIT EARNED", or "2 KEYS TO GO · DRILL G: 3 MORE", "1 KEY TO GO · MELODY F: LEARN ONE", and so on. <strong>Only a code taken when the checklist reads FULL CREDIT EARNED earns credit.</strong> The game will hand you a code before that, but it says so right above the code: "This code does NOT earn credit yet ... it grades as 0 until you finish." Don't submit that one. Come back for a new code when the checklist is green.</p>
<p>To submit: press <strong>GET CODE</strong>, type your first name, last name and Canvas ID (the number on your Canvas profile; your Orbit pilot name is not used), press GET CODE again, then <strong>COPY CODE</strong> and paste it into the <strong>Text Entry</strong> tab. Paste only the code, nothing else in the box, and paste all of it, from KTS1 to the last letter.</p>
<p>The code certifies the right notes in a steady rhythm at the Slow tempo in all three keys, plus the drills. It says nothing about fingering; nothing in this assignment does.</p>

<h4>Due Friday, September 25, 2026, 6:00 PM Eastern.</h4>
<h4>Lab hours (Room 269)</h4>
<p>Room 269 is open for practice at select hours this week. Check the posted schedule for exact times. Every keyboard there is full-size, MIDI-capable, and plugs into a computer. Cables provided, for either option.</p>
