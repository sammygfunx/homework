HW #7  Melody in Scale Degrees — RETIRED for Fall 2026 (Sam, 2026-09-16;
       unpublished in Canvas before any student started; replaced by HW
       #5 above). Kept here in case a class melody bank comes back later
       in the semester. The Fall 2025 text, for the record:
         "HW #7: Melody in Scale Degrees - due Thursday, September 17th
         by 11:59pm. After working through the Simple Melodies for
         Harmonization handout, think about a melody you love and would
         like to contribute to the class. The melody can be from any
         genre, but try to pick something fun that most would recognize.
         You could even pick a theme from a popular TV show, movie, or
         pop song. Alternatively, you could challenge yourself to find a
         notated melody (perhaps from your current classical/jazz
         repertoire) and translate the melody notes into scale degrees.
         You only need to notate scale degrees (numbers) and correct
         rhythms. Examples will be given in class, and there is space on
         the 2nd page of the handout to write out your melody, or feel
         free to use a sheet of paper and scan/upload. To receive full
         credit, the file you submit should: 1) include your name; 2)
         include the title of the piece/song the melody comes from; 3)
         include the artist's name (do not contribute your own original
         melody); 4) if you transcribed this melody by ear, include a
         YouTube URL in the submission comments so students can listen
         and practice along; 5) if you transcribed it from a written
         score, include a copy of the score used (URL link) in addition
         to your submission."
       The Coursepack's four redesign options (Clef Runner melody
       assignment, a scale-degree hearing game, a class melody bank, a
       6A/6B-style split) are in —Coursepack/docs/project_state.txt
       Pass 13, "HOMEWORK 7 ALTERNATIVES".
