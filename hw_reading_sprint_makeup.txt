--- DESCRIPTION (paste into the Rich Content Editor) ---

<p><strong>MISSED A READING SPRINT? EARN IT BACK.</strong> If you missed one
or more Reading Sprints this semester, this is your chance to make them up.
The sprints themselves stay closed. Instead, you will do some extra reading
practice in Clef Runner, and I will EXCUSE missed sprints based on how much
you read. Excused sprints no longer count against your grade.</p>

<p><strong>Turned in every sprint?</strong> You can skip this. It only
replaces missed sprints; it cannot raise a sprint you already turned in.</p>

<p><strong>WHAT TO DO</strong></p>
<ol>
<li>Open Clef Runner: <a href="https://clef-runner.netlify.app">https://clef-runner.netlify.app</a>
and choose the same player you always use.</li>
<li>Read in <strong>Sight-Reading Mode</strong> and/or the <strong>Infinite
Runner</strong> (both are on the main menu; the Homework screen's
<strong>Reading Sprint Make-Up</strong> card has a button for each). Sight-Reading
Mode is the random sight-reading; the Schäfer exercises (under
<strong>Repertoire &amp; Sight-Reading Exercises</strong>) do not count here. Mix
the two any way you like, in as many sittings as you like. One hand or both
hands, any level, any tempo: it all counts.</li>
<li>You need a <strong>MIDI keyboard</strong>, or an acoustic piano with
Clef Runner's microphone option (<strong>Settings</strong>, Input:
<strong>Microphone</strong>). Clef Runner has no on-screen keys.</li>
<li>Only runs you finish with at least <strong>70% of the notes right</strong>
count, so read carefully rather than quickly. Only practice from now on
counts; earlier practice was not recorded.</li>
</ol>

<p><strong>HOW MANY SPRINTS YOU EARN BACK</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>10 reading minutes:</strong> up to 3 missed sprints excused</li>
<li><strong>20 reading minutes:</strong> up to 6 missed sprints excused</li>
<li><strong>30 reading minutes:</strong> ALL your missed sprints excused</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>TO SUBMIT</strong></p>
<p>In Clef Runner, press <strong>Homework</strong> on the main menu. The
<strong>Reading Sprint Make-Up</strong> card is at the top and shows your
reading minutes; open it to see how many sprints they excuse. When you are
done, fill in your first name, last name and student ID, press
<strong>Get Code</strong>, then <strong>Copy Code</strong>, and paste the code
(it starts with KTS1-) into the text box below. Paste exactly ONE code. The
code always includes everything you have done so far. You can check your
minutes and take a code any time; if you read more later, take a new code and
resubmit. I'll use your last one.</p>

<p><strong>Due Monday 11/30 at 11:59 pm</strong> (last day of classes). No
late submissions.</p>

--- END DESCRIPTION ---

SECTION 4. DESIGN NOTES + FOR SAM
===========================================================
WHY MINUTES, NOT XP OR LEVELS: character XP and Sight-Reading levels already
include everything a student played before the offer, and XP also comes from
repertoire and scale play, so they can't separate new reading from old.
Minutes logged from the ship date, with a 70% floor, are one number that is
fair to every student. Students still level up their character as they
play; it just isn't what the grade reads.

DECIDED (Sam 2026-11-16):
  1. Tiers 10 / 20 / 30 minutes -> 3 / 6 / all, 70% floor: OK. One-hand runs
     and any difficulty count (students choose their level; the random
     sight-reading speeds up or slows down with their accuracy).
  2. No video way: the make-up path is already a kindness.
  3. 0 points. The hub reports who submitted, reading minutes, overall
     accuracy and the tier; Sam excuses that many missing sprints.
