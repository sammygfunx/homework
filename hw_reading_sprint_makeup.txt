               in the INFINITE RUNNER, counted only for runs where at least

               20 reading minutes  -> up to 6 missed sprints excused
               30 reading minutes  -> ALL missed sprints excused
               (A sprint is about 2 minutes of work; 30 minutes covers a
               semester's worth of missed sprints, as Sam asked.)

--- DESCRIPTION (paste into the Rich Content Editor) ---

<p><strong>MISSED A READING SPRINT? EARN IT BACK.</strong> If you missed one
or more Reading Sprints this semester, this is your chance to make them up.
The sprints themselves stay closed. Instead, you will do some extra reading
practice in Clef Runner, and I will EXCUSE missed sprints based on how much
you read. Excused sprints no longer count against your grade.</p>

<p><strong>Turned in every sprint?</strong> You can skip this. It only
replaces missed sprints; it cannot raise a sprint you already turned in.</p>

<p><strong>WHAT TO DO</strong></p>
<ol>
<li>Open Clef Runner: <a href="https://clef-runner.netlify.app">https://clef-runner.netlify.app</a>
and choose the same player you always use.</li>
<li>Read in <strong>Sight-Reading Mode</strong> (the random sight-reading,
not the Schäfer exercises) and/or the <strong>Infinite Runner</strong>. Mix
them any way you like, in as many sittings as you like.</li>
<li>Only runs where you get at least <strong>70% of the notes right</strong>
count, so read carefully rather than quickly. Only practice from today on
counts.</li>
</ol>

<p><strong>HOW MANY SPRINTS YOU EARN BACK</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>10 reading minutes:</strong> up to 3 missed sprints excused</li>
<li><strong>20 reading minutes:</strong> up to 6 missed sprints excused</li>
<li><strong>30 reading minutes:</strong> ALL your missed sprints excused</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>TO SUBMIT</strong></p>
<p>In Clef Runner, open the <strong>Homework</strong> screen, choose
<strong>Reading Sprint Make-Up</strong>, and check your reading minutes. When
you are done, press <strong>Get Code</strong>, then <strong>Copy Code</strong>,
and paste the code (it starts with KTS1-) into the text box below. Paste
exactly ONE code. The code always includes everything you have done so far,
so take it once at the end.</p>

<p><strong>Due Monday 11/30 at 11:59 pm</strong> (last day of classes). No
late submissions.</p>

--- END DESCRIPTION ---

SECTION 4. DESIGN NOTES + FOR SAM
===========================================================
WHY MINUTES, NOT XP OR LEVELS: character XP and Sight-Reading levels already
include everything a student played before the offer, and XP also comes from
repertoire and scale play, so they can't separate new reading from old.
Minutes logged from the ship date, with a 70% floor, are one number that is
fair to every student. Students still level up their character as they
play; it just isn't what the grade reads.
