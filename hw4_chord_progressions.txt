WHAT THE GAME CERTIFIES AND WHAT IT CANNOT — say it plainly, everywhere
this assignment is described. MIDI carries pitch, velocity and timing;
it carries NO finger data and NO hand assignment. Progression Pond's
judge (ChordJudge.gd) checks the sounding pitch-class set, the bass
note when the challenge requires it (every spring does), whether a
seventh chord's 5th may be omitted, and a 120 ms settle window for
"together". It never reads note-off timing, velocity, or which hand.
So Option B certifies: the correct chord, in the correct inversion,
played together, in order, in all three keys, with an honest count of
wrong chords and time. It cannot certify fingering or hands together.
Option A is the ONLY place fingering is graded, and the description's
"Which option should I pick?" paragraph says so in the student's
terms. Full argument: Progression_Pond/docs/hw4_design.txt.
