<h3>Quiz 6: the twelve major scales, with their fingering. Take it in Scale Fingering.</h3>
<p>This quiz replaces the old written scale quiz. For each of the twelve major scales, you show two things, left hand first and then right hand, two octaves going up: <strong>the image of the scale</strong> (tap its keys on the keyboard; the black keys show as dots) and <strong>its fingering</strong> (the finger number for each of the 15 notes, lowest note first).</p>
<p>Open it here: <a href="https://scale-fingering.netlify.app/?quiz=1">https://scale-fingering.netlify.app/?quiz=1</a>. It works on a phone, a tablet or a computer, with a mouse, your finger or the typing keyboard. You do not need a MIDI keyboard, and there is no MIDI in the quiz. It is open book: the Scale Fingering Chart in the book is the answer.</p>
<p>Want to practice first? <a href="https://scale-fingering.netlify.app">https://scale-fingering.netlify.app</a> has games for the groupings, where the thumb crosses and where finger 4 lands, a speed round and a grid of all twelve keys for each hand. Practice never counts for a grade.</p>

<h4>How to take it</h4>
<ol>
<li>Open the quiz link. Type your first name, last name and student ID. Use the student ID your gradebook knows you by.</li>
<li>The scales come in order: C, G, F, D, B-flat, A, E-flat, E, A-flat, B, D-flat, F-sharp/G-flat. For each one, do the left hand, then the right hand: tap the 15 keys of the scale, then tap (or type, with the digits 1 to 5) its 15 fingers, lowest note first. Tap a finger box to change it.</li>
<li>Use Prev and Next to move between scales. Your work is saved on your device, so a reload or turning your phone will not lose it.</li>
<li>Press <strong>Finish and get my code</strong>, then <strong>Copy code</strong>. Paste ONLY the code, all of it, from KTS1 to the last letter, under <strong>Text Entry</strong> here.</li>
</ol>

<h4>How it is graded</h4>
<p>Every scale counts the same. In each scale, each hand is worth half. Within a hand, the notes are half and the fingering is half, and each of the 15 notes counts the same. A key you leave out or a key that is not in the scale is a note wrong; a finger you leave blank is wrong. Partial credit adds up note by note, so a scale with one slip is nearly full marks. The fingering in the book's chart is the standard. Two places on the chart show a second correct choice in parentheses (B major left hand, the lowest B; F major right hand, the top F); either is right, and the app shows an alternative in parentheses so you know. A finger the chart does not show is wrong, even if you could play it.</p>

<h4>Take it as many times as you like. Your best code counts.</h4>
<p>You have unlimited attempts. The results screen shows your best code on that device; <strong>paste your best code</strong>. There is no video or paper way to take this quiz, so use your own phone or tablet, or a computer in the lab.</p>

<h4>Due Friday, November 13, 2026, 11:59 PM Eastern. We take it together in class that day.</h4>
