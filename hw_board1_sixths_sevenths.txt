<p>Record yourself at the piano playing these <strong>twelve intervals</strong> as blocked intervals (both notes together), and <strong>say the name of each one out loud</strong> as you play it, for example <em>"C up to A, major 6th."</em> Above C, then above F, then above G, play a major 6th, a minor 6th, a major 7th and a minor 7th.</p>
<p>Keep your hands and the keyboard in the frame and your voice clear. One take is fine. <strong>To submit:</strong> upload the video file under <strong>File Upload</strong>, or paste a link to an unlisted YouTube video under <strong>Text Entry</strong>. No cloud drive links.</p>
