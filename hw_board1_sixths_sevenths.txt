<h4>OPTION 3: Piano Tennis Gauntlet (alone or with a partner)</h4>
<ul>
<li>You need a MIDI keyboard (the class pianos work). The computer keyboard cannot reach a 6th or 7th from every root. On a computer, open <a href="https://piano-tennis.netlify.app">https://piano-tennis.netlify.app</a> in Chrome or Edge.</li>
<li>Choose <strong>VS COMPUTER</strong> to play alone, or <strong>LOCAL 1v1</strong> to play a partner on the same computer: one keyboard split at E4 and F4 (the left player plays E4 and below), or two MIDI keyboards.</li>
<li>Set <strong>Drill</strong> to <strong>Gauntlet</strong>, press <strong>OPEN THE GAUNTLET</strong>, then press <strong>PLAY STAGE 1</strong>. Gauntlet stays grayed out until the game finds a MIDI keyboard; plug one in and play a key.</li>
<li>There are four stages in order: minor 6th, major 6th, minor 7th, major 7th. Alone, each stage has a new computer opponent. With a partner, your partner is the opponent.</li>
<li>Play the interval up from a new root each time; the lower note is the root. A root counts only when your answer on it wins the point: you play it, return the ball, and your opponent misses. Your strip at the top shows the roots you have won.</li>
<li>A stage is cleared when you have won a point from each of the 12 roots, whatever the score. With a partner, each of you has your own strip, and the stage ends when you both have all 12.</li>
<li>Cleared stages are saved on that computer and browser, so you can stop between stages and press <strong>CONTINUE</strong> later.</li>

Enter your own name and school ID, press <strong>COPY CODE</strong>, and paste the whole code under <strong>Text Entry</strong> in your own Canvas. If you clear more stages later, submit a new code; your last submission is the one graded.</li>
</ul>

<h4>OPTION 4: Video</h4>
<ul>
<li>Record yourself playing major and minor 7ths as blocked intervals (both notes together), and name each one out loud: "C, major 7th. C, minor 7th."</li>
<li>On each root, play the major 7th, then move the top note down a half step for the minor 7th. Start on middle C and ascend chromatically to the C an octave higher, then descend chromatically back to middle C (25 roots, 50 sevenths). A black-key root may be named either way, such as C-sharp or D-flat.</li>
<li>Set a metronome to 60 or slower, loud enough to hear on the video. Play each seventh on the first click and say its name while you hold it for four clicks.</li>
<li>Record one continuous take with no edits; either hand is fine. Keep your hand and the keyboard in the frame.</li>
<li><strong>To submit:</strong> upload the video under <strong>File Upload</strong>, or paste a link to an unlisted YouTube video under <strong>Text Entry</strong>. Please do not send cloud drive links.</li>
</ul>
